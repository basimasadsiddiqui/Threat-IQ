# Brag Plan: ThreatIQ — the ad

## What is this app?
An agentic AI security operations platform. One box takes a URL, domain, IP,
file hash, CVE or a whole phishing email; a graph of specialist agents calls the
real threat-intel APIs, and a deterministic engine returns a scored, framework-
mapped, prioritised answer.

## The angle
The other two cuts are a thesis and a walkthrough. This one is an **advert**, and
an advert has a different job: it is watched by someone who has not asked for it,
so it must state a problem they recognise inside three seconds and end with
somewhere to go.

The problem is not "security is hard". It is the specific, physical thing an
analyst does: **one indicator, eleven browser tabs.** That opening image is the
whole pitch, because everyone who has triaged an alert has lived it.

Then the counter-image: eleven tabs collapse into one field. Then three proof
cards. Then the differentiator that no competitor will put on a slide — the tool
telling you what it could not check. Then the URL.

What this ad refuses to do: claim speed multipliers, say "powered by AI", or
imply the model decides anything. The product's own thesis is that the model does
not decide, and an advert that contradicts the product is worse than no advert.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Eleven browser tabs appear one after another across the top of the frame, fast,
each labelled with a real tool an analyst actually opens: VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB,
urlscan, NVD, CISA KEV, RDAP, whois, MXToolbox, Shodan, Talos, Hybrid Analysis.
Over them, one line: **One indicator. Eleven tabs.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- The tabs collapse into a single input field. `One box.`
- Three proof cards, arriving one at a time: **15 tools · 4 agents in parallel ·
  7-factor score**. Real numbers from the codebase, not marketing rounding.
- The differentiator card: `Scored on 56% of the model's weight.` with the line
  that explains why that is a feature — a missing lookup is not evidence of
  safety.
- One line of positioning, held: **Tools collect facts. The model interprets
  them.**

## Outro / punchline
The wordmark, the live URL, and `Free. No key required to start.` — which is
true: nine of the fifteen tools need no key at all.

## User flow worth showing
Compressed rather than absent: the input field is real, the score and the
coverage line are from a real run. The full flow is the job of the walkthrough
cut; this one shows the shape of it and sends people to the live URL to do it
themselves.

## Tone
- Preset: `app-store`
- Creative direction: a confident B2B security ad that never oversells, because
  the product's credibility IS the pitch
- Interpretation: clean cards, decisive cuts, one claim per frame. Numbers do the
  persuading. No adjectives where a figure will do. Motion arrives fast and stops
  dead — nothing drifts, nothing pulses.

## Format: square — 1080x1080
## Duration: 21.0 seconds

- Severity: critical `#ea5459`, high `#f76808`, low `#46a758`

## Share copy (draft)
One indicator used to mean eleven tabs. ThreatIQ takes a URL, domain, IP, hash,
CVE or a whole phishing email, runs four specialist agents across fifteen tools,
and returns a scored, framework-mapped answer with the remediation ranked.
Nine of those tools need no API key, so it works the moment you open it.

## Audio direction
- Role: clean product bed with crisp interface accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: bed at 0.24 set on the timeline, fade out under the URL
- Music cue guidance: strong cues at **3.70s** (the collapse), **7.92s** (first
  proof card), **12.65s** (the differentiator), **19.48s** (the URL). Proof cards
  on the beat grid at 7.92 / 8.96 / 10.01 — every other beat, since 0.52s
  spacing outruns reading a card.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. An advert that pulses with the music looks
  cheap and this product's pitch is credibility.
- SFX posture: crisp but sparse — a short tick per tab as they stack, one soft
  impact on the collapse, one tick per proof card, nothing under the closing
  line or the URL.
- Restraint rule: no risers, no whooshes, no stingers. The last four seconds
  carry no SFX at all.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Eleven tabs — 3.7s
Square frame. Eleven small tab chips stack in a grid, fast, each a real tool
name. Beneath them, large: **One indicator.** then **Eleven tabs.** arriving
second so the line lands as a complaint rather than a list.
Sequential/interaction: yes — eleven chips, roughly every 0.16s, fast enough to
read as an accumulating pile rather than eleven things to read individually.
Audio intent: a short dry tick per chip, accelerating slightly. Mildly irritating
on purpose; it is the problem.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — One box — 3.6s
Beat-locked to **3.70s**: the chips collapse inward and vanish, replaced by the
console's real input field with its actual label. Over it: **One box.**
The field shows `paypa1-secure-login.tk` already in it.
Sequential/interaction: yes — a collapse, then the field settling.
Audio intent: one soft impact as the chips land. Then quiet.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — What it does — 4.6s
Three proof cards arrive at **7.92 / 8.96 / 10.01**, each one number and one
line:
- `15 tools` / `nine need no API key`
- `4 agents` / `run in parallel, deterministically routed`
- `7 factors` / `weighted, inspectable, reproducible`
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards, one per other-beat, each with a tick.
Audio intent: one clean tick per card. Methodical.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The part nobody advertises — 4.4s
Beat-locked **12.65s**. A single wide card, severity-neutral:
**Scored on 56% of the model's weight.**
Beneath it, muted: `A source it could not reach is reported as missing coverage,
never scored as zero. A failed lookup is not evidence of safety.`
Sequential/interaction: the figure first, the explanation second.
Audio intent: nothing. The silence marks it as the serious claim.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The rule — 2.6s
Centred, held: **Tools collect facts. The model interprets them.**
Sequential/interaction: none. One line, one hold.
Audio intent: bed only.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Where to go — 2.1s
Wordmark, then `threat-iq-project.streamlit.app` at **19.48s**, then muted
beneath: `Free. No key required to start.`
Audio intent: music fades to silence. No stinger.

**Music mood for this video:** clean, confident, unhurried.
**Audio summary:** an irritating little pile of ticks for the problem, one impact
as it collapses, three clean ticks for the proof, then deliberate silence for the
claim that matters and the address.
