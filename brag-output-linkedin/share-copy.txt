Paste a suspicious domain into ThreatIQ and watch the whole thing run: it
classifies the input, fans out to four specialist agents, correlates what comes
back, and returns a named report — 66.9/100 HIGH, brand impersonation, with the
remediation you should action first.

The part I am most pleased with is the line nobody usually ships: "Scored on 56%
of the model's weight." When a source could not be reached, ThreatIQ reports the
gap instead of scoring it as zero, because a missing lookup is not evidence of
safety.

Risk scores, framework mappings and remediation are computed by deterministic
code. The model writes the narrative around them. Tools collect facts, the model
interprets them.
